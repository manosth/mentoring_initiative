GRE and TOEFL Prep

This is a potentially incomplete set of resources to prepare for GRE/TOEFL. Since tests change, the information and resources that we have might be outdated, but they are a good start. Let us know if you have more material that would be helpful to add here.

GRE
  • First of all, prepare using the official resources and information.
  • There are three parts in a GRE exam, the quantitative, the verbal, and the essays. The quantitative includes math-related questions and is the most important. The essays are also important but not that much. On verbal it is OK as long as you don't get a horrible grade.
  • Quantitative
    • First prepare using Princeton Review (you can ask us to find access to it).
    • Then, you can also do the Manhattan Prep tests (50$).
  • Essays
    • Practice doing essays on GRE topics and grade them yourself and with your peers.
  • Verbal
    • Do a practice exam to understand the structure.
    • Practice words using flashcard apps like the Magoosh one.
TOEFL
  • Prepare using official resources that contain a few practice tests and sets.
  • There also exist online resources to prepare (free or not) that you can search for and find.
  • The essay and verbal preparation for GRE covers part of the TOEFL exam in the sense that TOEFL essays are much simpler.
